Al Ummah Now is an Islamic video and community platform. Upload videos, go live and post to your channel, and see what the community is sharing, in Arabic and English.

With the Al Ummah Now app you can:
• Watch videos from channels on Al Ummah Now
• Keep watching in picture-in-picture while you use other apps
• Cast videos to your TV
• Use the app in Arabic or English, with a light or dark theme

Links to videos on videos.alummahnow.net open in the app.
